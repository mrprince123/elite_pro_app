# Elite Fitness Pro 🏋️‍♂️✨

**Elite Fitness Pro** is a state-of-the-art mobile application designed to bring clinical precision to fitness tracking. Combining Material 3 principles with an "Approachable Medical" aesthetic, it acts as both a high-performance training log and a premium wellness companion.

---

## 🌟 Introduction

Elite Fitness Pro bridges the gap between raw health metrics and structured exercise training. Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, the app empowers users to monitor their physical activity, manage custom workout structures, follow expert-designed routines, and explore a deep catalog of movements. 

With native system integrations, Elite Fitness Pro offers a unified health dashboard that responds directly to your body's live data.

---

## ⚡ Core Features

### 1. Unified Workouts Center
A single dashboard containing your entire fitness catalog:
*   **Custom Routines**: Build your own workout routines by selecting sets, reps, and target rest times for any exercise. Custom cards dynamically adapt their covers to match the targeted muscles or workout category.
*   **Prebuilt Plans**: Instantly follow expertly crafted training regimens scaled for different levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and muscle groups.

### 2. Live Health Connect Integration
Seamless integration with Google's Android system Health Connect platform:
*   **Live Syncing**: Connects with native device metrics to aggregate your physical telemetry.
*   **Comprehensive Activity Ring**: Tracks daily progress metrics including **Steps Today**, **Move Minutes (MINS)**, **Heart Points (HEART PTS)**, **Calories Burned (KCAL)**, and **Distance (KM)**.
*   **Android 14 Compatible**: Utilizes the modern system permission layer and intent-filter framework for reliable background reads.

### 3. Comprehensive Exercise Library
Browse through a large database of physical movements:
*   **Muscle & Equipment Filters**: Filter exercises by primary muscle groups or required gear (Dumbbells, Barbells, Bodyweight, etc.).
*   **Rich Media Detail**: View step-by-step instructions, benefits, common mistakes, and loops of exercises in action via images or video tutorials.

### 4. Interactive Workout Sessions
Track active routines in real-time:
*   **Rep & Set Tracking**: Log completed exercises during your session.
*   **Timer Utilities**: Follow rest intervals automatically to optimize muscle recovery.
*   **Active Dashboard Refreshes**: Automatically recalculates training volumes and updates the Home dashboard when a session is finalized.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design System

The application's design system follows a modern and clean aesthetic centered around readability, encouragement, and high geometry:
*   **Primary Theme**: Sleek dark modes, harmony-focused dark palettes, and frosted glass (Glassmorphism) container elevations.
*   **Brand Typography**: Clear structural hierarchies using premium modern typography (Inter, Outfit style) to optimize read-times.
*   **Functional Activity Color Coding**:
    *   🔵 **Blue**: Move Minutes / Distance
    *   🟢 **Green**: Daily Steps / Heart Points
    *   🟡 **Yellow**: Target Muscles / Equipment Category
    *   🔴 **Red**: Energy / Calories Burned / Advanced Intensity
